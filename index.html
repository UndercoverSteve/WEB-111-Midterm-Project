What is virtual reality?

Virtual reality is a computer generated, 3D simulated environment. Users wear a HMD (head mounted display) that has two high resolution screens that show slightly offset images to create depth.

What is it used for?

  • Gaming & Entertainment
    • Watching films
    • Social VR
    • Exploring places you've never been to
  • Education
    • Medical and surgical training
    • Vocational training for high risk jobs
    • Soft skills
    • Flight/driving training
  • Business
    • Meetings
