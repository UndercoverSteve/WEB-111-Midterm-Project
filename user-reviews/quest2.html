Quest 2 Review

The Quest 2 was released in October 2020 at a price point of $299.99 for its 64GB model, and $399 for its 256GB model. In 2021, Meta replaced the 64GB model for 128GB at the same starting price. In 2022, Meta raised prices across the board by $100 dollars, bringing the 128GB model to $399 and the 256GB model to $499.

It sold over 20 million units worldwide, ranking it the #24th best selling console of all time, just below the Nintendo GameCube. This is about 10x the amount that the Quest 3 sold as of 10/16/2026.

It was an affordable entry into VR, being an all in one headset. No external computer required.

Would I recommend this nowadays? Only if you can pick it up cheap. Like $100 or less.
